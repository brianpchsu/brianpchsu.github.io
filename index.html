About Me

Hi! I'm Brian Hsu — a Staff Software Engineer at a tech company in Silicon Valley, with a story that didn't start in a computer science classroom. My non-traditional pivot into tech has given me a firsthand understanding of what it takes to navigate career transitions, build software at scale, and lead with empathy. That journey also sparked a passion for community building: I run Pivot Software Engineer in Silicon Valley, where I share raw, first-hand career observations, and founded Taiwanese Referral Network — a referral network uniting over 400 overseas Taiwanese professionals to help talented people get better opportunities to apply, be seen, and find where they belong in the global tech industry.

關於我

你好！我是 Brian Hsu，目前在矽谷科技公司擔任 Staff Software Engineer。我的科技之路並非從 CS 本科開始——正是這段半路出家的歷程，讓我對跨領域轉職的挑戰有了深刻的切身體會，也形塑了我在做技術決策與協作時更具韌性與同理心的視角。我熱衷於把自己踩過的坑與累積的觀察，化為對他人有價值的資源：透過半路工程師在矽谷，我持續記錄矽谷職場的第一手洞察；而我發起的歹晚郎內推互助網絡，集結超過 400 位海外台灣人，致力於幫助更多優秀人才獲得更好的求職與被看見的機會，找到屬於自己的位置。
